Executive findings

1Best control
New Traffic Campaign delivered 138 installs at $1.08 CPI, the strongest measured acquisition economics.
2Budget leakage
$393.12 was spent on campaigns with no reported installs. This is either real waste or a material attribution failure.
3Weakest tracked campaign
New App promotion Campaign - April 2026 produced installs at $3.02 CPI, nearly 2.8× the best campaign.
4Scale constraint
The export lacks subscription revenue and audience segmentation. Profitability and audience winners cannot be validated.

Prioritized action plan

01 · Measurement
Fix paid conversion tracking
Track subscription purchase, revenue, renewal, cancellation, and retention. Optimize Meta toward the deepest reliable event.
02 · Reallocation
Pause unmeasured spend
Do not fund campaigns with clicks but no reported installs until attribution is reconciled against App Store and product analytics.
03 · Testing
Use the winner as control
Keep the $1.08 CPI campaign unchanged and test materially different hooks, demonstrations, and proof-led creatives.
04 · Scaling
Scale in controlled steps
After paid economics are verified, increase the winner 15–25% every 2–3 days while monitoring CAC and downstream conversion.

Statistical interpretation and data limitations

CTR differences can be evaluated directionally, but this campaign-level export does not contain independent creative variants, audience rows, subscriptions, purchases, conversion value, or ROAS. Therefore, claims about best audience or highest-ROAS creative would be unsupported. Meta attribution and repeated impressions also violate strict independence assumptions used in simple significance tests.
