01 · SCALE

Make Reels 50% of output

Use 15–35 second screen-led videos with the product visible in the first three seconds. Lead with a route decision, hidden risk, or concrete number.

02 · REPEAT

Build around workflow proof

Expand ForeFlight import, raw-briefing versus organized-output, and real-route breakdowns. Specific functionality materially outperformed broad AI claims.

03 · CONVERT

Use one low-friction CTA

Match the action to the objective: follow for discovery, save for education, comment for scenarios, and trial only after the value is demonstrated.

04 · STOP

Reduce generic static promotions

Static benefit lists had weak distribution and low action. Replace slogans with actual screens, operational examples, and pilot decision tension.

05 · CADENCE

Publish consistently for six weeks

Target four weekly feed posts: two Reels, one carousel, and one demonstration or founder/user story. Avoid bursts followed by silence.

06 · MEASURE

Connect content to activation

Add profile visits, link clicks, app installs, first briefing completed, and subscription conversion. Engagement without activation is not business performance.
